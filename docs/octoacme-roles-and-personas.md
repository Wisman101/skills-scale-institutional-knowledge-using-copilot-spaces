# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. They gather, document, and clarify requirements from the business side, translating them into actionable tasks that developers can implement while ensuring expectations are clearly set with all stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into technical specifications and user stories
- Collaborate with Product Manager and Project Manager to define acceptance criteria
- Facilitate requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Maintain requirements documentation and traceability matrices
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs
- Support UAT (User Acceptance Testing) activities

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable
- Minimize rework due to misunderstood or incomplete requirements
- Facilitate effective communication between business and technical teams
- Support timely and accurate project delivery

### Typical Communication
- Requirements review sessions with stakeholders
- Story refinement meetings with developers
- Weekly alignment with Product Manager and Project Manager
- Documentation updates and requirements clarifications

---

## Risk Manager

### Role Summary
Risk Managers proactively identify, evaluate, and mitigate project risks. They work closely with all team members to monitor the risk register, escalate issues appropriately, and ensure risk response plans are executed effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project risks throughout the project lifecycle
- Maintain and update the risk register with current risk status
- Develop and implement risk mitigation and contingency plans
- Monitor risk indicators and trigger points
- Facilitate risk review sessions during project meetings
- Escalate critical risks to appropriate stakeholders
- Track risk response actions to completion
- Document lessons learned related to risk management

### Goals
- Minimize negative impact of risks on project objectives
- Enable proactive rather than reactive risk management
- Maintain visibility of risks across all stakeholders
- Build organizational risk management capability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly risk review updates at team syncs
- Risk escalation notifications to Project Manager and Sponsor
- Monthly risk summary reports to stakeholders
- Ad-hoc risk alerts for emerging high-impact issues

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads oversee the quality assurance process across all project phases. They ensure proper test planning and execution, establish quality standards, and work closely with developers to identify and resolve defects promptly.

### Responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive test strategies and plans
- Define quality metrics and acceptance criteria
- Lead test case design and review
- Coordinate testing activities across team members
- Manage defect tracking and resolution
- Perform or oversee integration and end-to-end testing
- Ensure test automation coverage meets standards
- Validate releases meet quality gates before deployment
- Report on quality metrics and testing progress

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, defect-free releases
- Minimize production defects and customer-reported issues
- Ensure test coverage is comprehensive and maintainable
- Foster a quality-first culture across the team

### Typical Communication
- Daily coordination with developers on defect resolution
- Test status updates in sprint reviews and standups
- Quality gate assessments before releases
- Test plan reviews with Project Manager and Product Manager

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide overarching direction and champion the project's strategic value within the organization. They secure necessary resources, make high-level decisions, and act as an escalation point for major issues that require executive attention.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate project vision and strategic alignment
- Secure funding and resources for the project
- Provide executive-level guidance and decision-making
- Remove organizational barriers and obstacles
- Champion the project to senior leadership and stakeholders
- Approve major scope, schedule, or budget changes
- Review project status at key milestones
- Ensure project delivers expected business value

### Goals
- Align project outcomes with organizational strategy
- Enable project success through adequate resourcing
- Maintain executive visibility and support
- Ensure return on investment is achieved

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive status reviews with Project Manager
- Milestone review meetings and gate decisions
- Escalation discussions for critical issues
- Stakeholder alignment meetings

---

## Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are individuals or groups affected by the project outcome. They provide essential feedback, review progress, and help ensure deliverables meet business needs. Stakeholders may represent various business units, end users, customers, or support teams.

### Responsibilities
- Provide input on requirements and priorities
- Review and provide feedback on project deliverables
- Participate in acceptance testing when applicable
- Communicate business needs and constraints
- Support change management and adoption activities
- Escalate concerns or issues to Project Manager
- Validate that deliverables meet expectations

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers value to their area or user group
- Minimize disruption from project changes
- Provide timely feedback to keep project on track
- Support successful adoption of project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Participation in requirements gathering sessions
- Monthly stakeholder update meetings
- Demo and review sessions at sprint/milestone completions
- Ad-hoc feedback channels (emails, surveys, feedback forms)

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set of personas ensures clear accountability and reduces ambiguity in project workflows.
- These roles work together to create a comprehensive project team structure that addresses requirements gathering, risk management, quality assurance, and stakeholder alignment.
